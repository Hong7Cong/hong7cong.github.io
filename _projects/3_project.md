**Dec 2024 – Present** · Signal Analysis and Interpretation Laboratory (SAIL), USC

Body-worn cameras record traffic stops from the officer's point of view. This creates a large, egocentric record of real-world interactions between officers and civilians. This project, run in partnership with law enforcement, builds video pipelines to analyze these interactions at scale.

#### Highlights

- Designed a video embedding extraction pipeline and setting-selection process over **30K+ body-worn videos** of traffic stops.
- Analyzing **2K+ annotated videos** of multimodal, collaborative officer-civilian activity from multiple views.
