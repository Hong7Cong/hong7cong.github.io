**Sep 2025 – Mar 2026** · In partnership with Capital One

How do people perceive trust and emotion in multimodal content, and how well do models capture those perceptions? This project collected human judgments on advertisements. It then benchmarked models against them and studied how reliable the human labels are.

#### Highlights

- Collected and benchmarked the **EVALUATE** dataset (920 ads), exposing gaps in how models capture emotion and trust.
- Showed via label-stability analysis that survey design systematically shifts human annotator judgments.
